## Known Issues
- The `ClimateSkillScore.compute()` method uses a reference-variance FSS formulation;
  at very small thresholds with all-zero fields this may produce NaN results (clamped to 0).
- `on_train_batch_start` in `LatentDiffusion` references `self.restarted_from_ckpt` which may
  not be defined when `scale_by_std` is True — currently a no-op in typical usage.
- Resuming from checkpoint may trigger multiple `setup()` calls on the DataModule;
  Lightning handles this, but the data loader re-scans all files each time.
- Validation/test sampling uses the ground-truth future average intensity as the alignment target
  (oracle KA, as in PreDiff), so KA scores are optimistic vs. operational forecasting, where the
  target is estimated from the input context.
- The alignment guidance is now a per-sample |pred - target| instead of a joint L2 norm over the batch
  and lead times; its gradient magnitude differs, so `guide_scale` may need re-tuning.
- `data.split.mode: random` (default) still splits overlapping sliding windows randomly, which leaks
  frames between train/val/test; prefer `mode: year` for reported results.
- Forecast locates context files by sorted filename and does not check temporal contiguity
  (the training loader does); gaps in the data directory are not detected at forecast time.
